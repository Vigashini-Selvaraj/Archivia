JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

Eras of Literature

800 BC

Antiquity

The dawn of epic poetry, foundational philosophy, and early drama. This era laid the groundwork for all Western literature, introducing heroes, gods, and the very structure of narrative.

Homer Plato Aristotle Sophocles
14th C.

The Renaissance

A rebirth of humanism and art, propelled by the revolution of the printing press. Literature shifted towards the human experience, challenging old dogmas with wit and observation.

Dante Alighieri William Shakespeare Miguel de Cervantes
19th C.

Romanticism & Realism

A reaction against extreme rationalism, giving rise to intense emotion, individualism, and nature worship, eventually transitioning into the gritty, unvarnished depiction of everyday life.

Mary Shelley Victor Hugo Jane Austen Fyodor Dostoevsky
