Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my Premium membership?
You can cancel your membership at any time by going to your Account Settings, clicking on the "Billing" tab, and selecting "Cancel Subscription". You will retain access until the end of your billing cycle.
Can I read offline?
Yes! Premium members can save up to 50 books for offline reading. Simply click the "Download" icon next to any book in the library while you are connected to the internet.
Why isn't the reader formatting correctly on my tablet?
Ensure you are using the latest version of your web browser. If the issue persists, try clearing your browser cache or tapping the "Aa" icon in the reader to reset the typography settings to default.
How do I submit an article for the Blog?
We are always looking for fresh editorial voices! Please use the contact form above and select "Editorial Inquiry". Include a brief pitch and a link to your previous work.
