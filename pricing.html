SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any point. Your access will remain active until the end of your current billing period, after which you will revert to the Free plan.
What happens to my saved books if I cancel?
Your reading history and saved shelves are permanently tied to your account. If you cancel your premium plan, you won't be able to read the full books, but your curated lists will remain intact for when you decide to return.
Can I read offline?
Yes! Both the Reader and Unlimited plans allow you to download books to your device so you can read them without an active internet connection.
What is included in the Historical Archives?
The Unlimited plan unlocks our specialized collection of digitized historical manuscripts, rare editorial prints from the 19th and 20th centuries, and academic journals that are not available in the standard library.
